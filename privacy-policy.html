Privacy Policy

Home Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2, 2026

At Dr. Mukhtars Imaging Centre we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit www.drmukhtarsimaging.com, book an appointment, contact us, or use any of our services.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

When you contact us or book an appointment, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Age and Gender (where applicable)
  • Preferred Appointment Date & Time
  • Imaging Service Required
  • Doctor Referral Information (if provided)
  • Any information you voluntarily submit through forms or WhatsApp.

Medical Information

Where necessary for providing diagnostic services, we may collect:

  • Medical history relevant to your imaging examination
  • Previous reports or prescriptions
  • Referral notes from your doctor

Medical information is handled with strict confidentiality.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visit
  • Website usage statistics
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Provide diagnostic imaging services
  • Communicate regarding appointments or reports
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain internal records
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Prevent fraud or misuse of our services

We only collect information that is necessary for providing healthcare and improving patient experience.

3. Medical Confidentiality

Patient privacy is extremely important to us. All medical records, imaging reports, scans, and personal health information are treated as confidential. Access is limited to authorised medical professionals and staff who require the information to provide healthcare services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following situations:

  • With treating physicians or healthcare providers (with your consent or where medically necessary)
  • With diagnostic consultants involved in your care
  • When required by applicable law or court order
  • To protect legal rights or patient safety
  • With trusted service providers who assist in operating our website or appointment systems under confidentiality obligations

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to:

  • Improve website performance
  • Remember user preferences
  • Analyse website traffic
  • Enhance user experience

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access
  • Disclosure
  • Alteration
  • Loss
  • Misuse

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary:

  • To provide healthcare services
  • To comply with applicable medical record retention laws
  • For legal and regulatory requirements

Once information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally permissible
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise concerns regarding the handling of your personal information

Requests may be submitted using the contact details below.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website may include links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services may be used for patients under 18 years of age only under the supervision or consent of a parent, guardian, or legally authorized representative where required.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or services. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
