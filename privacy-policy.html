Privacy Policy

Home Privacy Policy

1. Clinical Data Protection

At Dr. Mukhtar's Imaging Centre, we prioritize the confidentiality and security of our patients' medical records and diagnostic images. We ensure that all scans, prescription uploads, and clinical data are processed in compliance with local medical safety guidelines and Indian healthcare privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect patient names, contact numbers, doctor referrals, scan dates, and clinical messages when scheduling diagnostics or pathology tests. This information is utilized solely to facilitate bookings and communicate diagnostic preparation steps.

3. Data Access & Sharing

Your diagnostic reports and images are only shared with authorized medical professionals involved in your care. We do not sell or lease patient records to third-party marketing networks.

4. WhatsApp Communications

Our appointment booking system forwards requests to WhatsApp. By initiating a booking query, you consent to communicate with our clinic representatives regarding your schedule, diagnostic preparation guidelines, and scan reports.

5. Contacting Us

For questions regarding this policy or to request access to your diagnostic records, please reach out to our administration desk at umarmukhtarsiddiqi@hotmail.com.
