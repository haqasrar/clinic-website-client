Our Profile

Pioneering Diagnostic Radiology in Srinagar

Dr. Mukhtars Imaging Centre was established with a singular vision: to bring world-class diagnostic clarity to the people of Jammu & Kashmir. Located centrally in Karan Nagar, Srinagar, opposite the Government Medical College, we have consistently introduced cutting-edge imaging technologies to the state.

Our clinic operates under a strict philosophy of accuracy, integrity, and swiftness. We understand that behind every scan is a patient seeking answers and a physician waiting to make vital treatment decisions.

With our recent introduction of **J&K's first Digital PET-CT scanner (uMI550)**, we have solidified our standing as the region's leading diagnostic hub, offering patients clinical options that match major metro diagnostic standards without leaving Srinagar.

Accreditations & Compliance

Strict Compliance & Safe Scanning

At Dr. Mukhtars Imaging Centre, patient safety is supported by strict adherence to national regulatory guidelines. Our diagnostic machines are fully registered, calibrated, and maintained:

  • AERB Certified: All radiation-emitting hardware is approved by the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB) of India.
  • Local Health Department Registration: Fully registered with the J&K State Health Services.
  • Safety Shields: Equipped with lead-shielded scan rooms and automated dosimeters to monitor exposure.
